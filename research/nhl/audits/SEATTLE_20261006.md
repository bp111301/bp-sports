## Why the input can be missing

`experiment_team_stats.py` sets a per-game power-play rate to null when a team has zero power-play opportunities. Its shifted 20-game rolling average requires at least three non-null values, and its season grouping resets each season. The home-away difference is null when either team's average is unavailable. Thus a small early-season sample can leave this feature absent even while shots, scoring and other features are present. An incomplete source row could also produce missing values.

The saved input proves that the feature was missing. The exact underlying per-game power-play rows from the original morning forecast were not persisted with that watchlist row. The live NHL Stats REST endpoint returned HTTP 403 during this audit; the exact zero-opportunity/missing-row explanation could not be verified. Do not present that mechanism as a proven source-data error. The full feature snapshot used here was captured later before the same game, not at the original morning watchlist capture.

## Scope and implications

This is a missing-feature sensitivity concern, not proof that all 90% predictions are wrong or that a loss alone establishes miscalibration. The training pipeline includes several correlated missingness indicators; an isolated missing power-play feature can behave very differently from all advanced features being absent. Any future feature treatment needs a separate, versioned candidate and honest evaluation. Do not cap probabilities or alter frozen code-hash manifests to silently change the current watchlist.
