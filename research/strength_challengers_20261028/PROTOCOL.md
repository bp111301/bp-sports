# Opponent-adjusted strength challenger protocol — 2026-10-28

Registered before this experiment's results. Previous experiments reused these development seasons; these results are not an independent holdout or proof of live improvement. No search beyond the two recipes per sport below. Rejected recipes remain rejected; no post-result hyperparameter adjustment.

## Hypothesis and information boundary
Existing efficiency/rolling predictors may not fully capture opponent-adjusted scoring strength or nonlinear interactions. Add a ridge least-squares team-strength difference reconstructed before every game, using only completed source games dated at least two calendar days earlier and within 365 days. Same-day and previous-day games are excluded conservatively. Use stable team IDs, not names. Fit weighted clipped score margins minus fixed home advantage against +1 home/-1 away team indicators, with zero-centered ridge prior. CFB: margin clip 42, home advantage 3 (zero at neutral sites), decay half-life 180 days, ridge penalty 10. NHL: clip 5, home advantage .2, half-life 90 days, penalty 20. The score feature is only the difference in fitted team coefficients. An unseen team has zero strength. No markets, realized QB fields, current-game labels, future labels, or 2026 live results enter feature fitting or candidate selection.

## Exactly two candidates per sport
1. `strength_linear`: CFB retains original context/prior k7 .25/.75 blend and C=.5 logistic recipes, adding strength difference to both component feature sets. NHL uses the original 18 features plus strength, C=.5 logistic with median imputation and no missingness indicators (known missingness sensitivity).
2. `strength_nonlinear50`: fixed 50% original control probability + 50% histogram gradient boosting on the original prior matrix plus strength (CFB), or original 18-feature matrix plus strength (NHL). Boosting: learning_rate=.05, max_iter=150, max_leaf_nodes=7, max_depth=3, min_samples_leaf=50, l2_regularization=10, max_bins=255, early_stopping=False, random_state=20261028. No randomized validation split or hyperparameter tuning.

CFB uses existing guarded 2015–2025 source, expanding training strictly earlier seasons, test 2018–2025 (5,733 games); control is original .25 context/.75 k7 prior. NHL uses existing guarded 2018–19 through 2024–25 sources, expanding training earlier seasons, test 2021–22 through 2024–25 (5,248 games); control original 18-feature indicator logistic. Exclude NHL 2025–26 and both live seasons. Recompute and verify same controls as earlier experiment.

## Research eligibility — not production promotion
Both Brier and log loss must improve against the same-game control. Accuracy must improve at least .5 percentage points CFB or .3 NHL. Brier must improve in at least 5/8 CFB or 3/4 NHL test seasons, with accuracy no worse across recent 2023–2025 CFB or 2023–24/2024–25 NHL seasons. Report per-season results and paired block-bootstrap accuracy difference intervals (CFB season/week, NHL game date, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261028). These intervals describe uncertainty on reused historical development data, not confirmation of a selected candidate. No feature importance-based recipe revisions after testing.

Retain only eligible candidates for a separately versioned prospective shadow implementation. No existing weight, forecast, historical evaluation, grading, or live builder is changed by this research. Before any future promotion, require a new frozen shadow specification, fresh pregame input provenance, and at least 200 new paired settled CFB games or 300 NHL games. Do not backfill shadow predictions for completed or started games. A negative result is published unchanged.
